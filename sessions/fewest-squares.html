Your investigation

Try it on the table
Cut squares from squared paper or card. Try several sizes and reuse the pieces.

No timer. Your goal is to improve a design and explain your choices. Different arrangements can be equally good.
For educators

The original challenge uses an 11 × 13 rectangle. The smaller boards are entry points. This companion uses squares aligned to the grid with whole-number side lengths. “Covered” does not mean optimal: no unproved minimum is labelled as the answer.

Start with the physical materials. Use the screen to inspect, compare or test a prediction. Keep one worthwhile question going; there is no timer or compulsory sequence.
