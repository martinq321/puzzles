15 meets noughts & crosses

Choose a number to claim its square. Every row, column and diagonal totals 15. Player 1 marks X; Player 2 marks O.

The top row is 8, 1, 6: those three cards form a winning line. Claimed squares stay visible here; both players’ cards also appear below.

Two games, the same choices

The eight lines of this magic square are exactly the eight triples of distinct numbers from 1–9 that total 15. So a win, block or fork has the same meaning in both games.

Use actual number cards

Lay cards 1–9 between you. Move chosen cards into each player’s hand. Use the screen to record moves if useful. Play several games before showing the magic square. A win uses exactly three cards from a hand, even if that hand contains four or five cards.

Take your time. Explain a choice. Try a variation.
