From paper to a universal idea

Use numbered squares and a counter as a tape and head. One person follows the rules literally; another writes the program. Can you explain why it stops?

A Turing machine has a tape, a reading head and a finite set of states. This explorer uses two states and a binary alphabet plus blank; its tape grows as needed. A 500-step pause prevents accidental endless runs.
