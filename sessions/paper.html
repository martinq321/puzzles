Tutor: preparation and next questions

Try the paperclip setup yourself before the session. Offer one surprise, then follow the learner’s questions rather than rush through every activity. These diagrams are schematic explanations, not a simulation of real paper.

For loops: mark a centre line before cutting. Trace the surface and boundary first. Change only the number of twists, and ask whether two loops are separate or linked.

For beams: use the same paper, span and load position. Compare a flat sheet, a folded channel and an accordion fold. Shape changes stiffness; this does not make the material itself stronger.

Take your time. Explain a choice. Try a variation.
