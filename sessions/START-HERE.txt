MATHS WEDNESDAYS — THREE HANDS-ON INVESTIGATIONS

Copy these three HTML files into your existing sessions folder:
  30-cubes.html
  fewest-squares.html
  36-fences.html

Keep their filenames unchanged. Each page links to the other two.
No separate CSS, JavaScript, images or internet connection are needed for the activities.
The Home link returns to ../index.html on your website.
Your existing homepage is not replaced or edited by this pack.

Open 30-cubes.html to begin, or open any of the pages directly.
Each includes Start / Explore / Go further prompts, optional hints,
physical-material suggestions, local notes, and a collection of saved designs.
Records save in the same browser on the same device; they do not sync between devices.
Download my record exports notes and design data as JSON for keeping a copy.

30 CUBES: dimension sliders and rotatable cuboids; 1–120 cubes;
rotated duplicates recognised; optional surface area.
FEWEST SQUARES: drag to draw a square, or choose a size and tap to place;
tap a piece to remove it; undo; boards from 3 x 5 to 11 x 13.
36 FENCES: start with rectangles, then edit individual grid edges;
12, 16, 24 or 36 pieces; optional area and saved comparisons.

The pages credit and link to the original Youcubed tasks.
These are independently built interactive companions.
