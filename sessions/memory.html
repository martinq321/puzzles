A moment for memory

A short starter, a shared pause or a finishing game. Turn over two cards and find the matching pair.

Ways to use it

No countdown and no speed score. In two-player mode, a match earns one point and another turn. Matched pairs move into the winner’s collection on the left or right. Empty spaces stay in place so other cards do not shift. After a mismatch, take as long as you want to look, then cover the cards to change turns.

For an extension, match equivalent fractions and decimals. Say why they agree before choosing the next pair. Begin with four pairs if you want a brief break.

Take your time. Explain a choice. Try a variation.
