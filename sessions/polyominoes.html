A small hint

Start with a line. Move one square. Try branching, or making a corner. Check for shapes that turn into each other.

Your collection

Choose a piece, then tap a square to place its top-left corner. You can also drag pieces from the tray or on the board.

Place by row and column

Pieces snap to the grid. Overlaps and placements outside the board are rejected.

Your pieces

Twist with two fingers to rotate a piece by quarter-turns, on the board or in the tray. Or use Rotate and Flip. Select a placed piece to move it.
