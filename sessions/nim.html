Take-away strategy

Take counters, leave a position, watch what happens. Can you explain a useful move?

Moves so far
    After experimenting: look for positions to leave

    This is a one-pile take-away game. Multi-pile Nim has different rules and a different strategy.

    Play with actual counters

    Start with 15 counters and allow 1–3 per move. Ask which position someone would like to receive when only a few remain. Work backwards from there. Change one rule at a time; a strategy for “last wins” may fail for “last loses”. Use the screen to track moves or check a conjecture.

    Take your time. Explain a choice. Try a variation.
