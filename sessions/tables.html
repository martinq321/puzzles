Times-table studio

Choose your tables, build a run of correct answers and watch your progress. Play at your own pace or try a timed round.

Choose your tables (at least one)

Settings apply to the next session. Timed rounds continue until time runs out; untimed sessions use the selected question count. Press Enter or Check to submit. Correct answers move on automatically by default.

For tutoring sessions

Choose one or two tables and five questions for a short start or finish. Incorrect answers can be retried. In timed rounds the clock continues while you think; choose Clock off for an untimed session. First-try independent answers are recorded separately from answers completed with hints or retries.
