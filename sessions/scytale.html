Make it with paper · and a note about the model

Wrap a narrow paper strip edge-to-edge around a dowel. Hold just the ends with small pieces of removable tape. Write along the rod, turn it to add another line, then unwrap. Your partner needs a matching rod and the same wrapping direction.

This is a simplified, evenly spaced model: the original rod has eight turns and four letter positions around each turn. A real strip has thickness and can slip. Colours are optional learning guides, not part of a secret code. Spaces and unused positions are both displayed as dots; keep the message length separately if that matters.

A scytale rearranges letters (transposition). A Caesar cipher replaces letters (substitution). These are investigation tools, not secure ways to protect real private messages.
