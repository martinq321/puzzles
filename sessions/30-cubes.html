What is a factor?

A factor of 30 divides 30 exactly. Every edge length in a 30-cube block is a factor of 30.

Cube or cuboid?

For educators · answers and prompts

The five cuboids, ignoring rotation, are 1×1×30, 1×2×15, 1×3×10, 1×5×6 and 2×3×5. Factors: 1, 2, 3, 5, 6, 10, 15, 30.

“Exactly 30” is the investigation’s only requirement. Other products remain visible for experimenting. Start with flat arrays to establish factor pairs, then build in three dimensions. There is no speed score.

For completeness, put dimensions in increasing order. The shortest edge can only be 1 or 2: if all were at least 3, the next possible product of three integer sides after 3×3×3 is at least 36. For shortest edge 1, list factor pairs of 30; for 2, the only ordered remaining pair is 3×5.

30 is not a cube number: 3³ = 27 and 4³ = 64. The earlier browser collection is copied when available, retaining valid 30-cube designs.
