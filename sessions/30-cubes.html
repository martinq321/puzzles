Use sugar cubes or wooden cubes. Start with 12; keep every cube in your solid cuboid.

Further challenge: choose any total from 1 to 120. Explore 24, 30, 36 or 60 cubes.
For educators

The source task explores totals 1–30. This companion adds larger totals and surface area. Dimension sliders construct solid cuboids rather than arbitrary piles. The model fits the viewing window automatically; compare dimensions, not apparent on-screen size. Wrapping area assumes each small cube has side length 1.

Start with the physical materials. Use the screen to inspect, compare or test a prediction. Keep one worthwhile question going; there is no timer or compulsory sequence.
