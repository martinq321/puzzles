What is happening?

The blue cube keeps the same orientation as it slides along a straight line. You are rotating your view of both cubes together. Its square face fits inside a square tunnel cut at an angle through the yellow cube.

This is a version of Prince Rupert’s cube. The mathematical construction allows a square of side 3√2 / 4 ≈ 1.06066 inside a unit cube. This model uses a slightly narrower tunnel so the remaining frame has thickness.

The see-through option is an inspection aid. Turn it off to see which surfaces genuinely hide others. This is an original mathematical model, not an imported copy of the video’s print file.
