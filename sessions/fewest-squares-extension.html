For educators

This extension adds larger coprime rectangles to the original 11 × 13 challenge. Coprime dimensions avoid a common scaling factor; they do not guarantee a harder puzzle. No minimum counts are claimed here. This companion uses squares aligned to the grid with whole-number side lengths. “Covered” does not mean optimal: no unproved minimum is labelled as the answer.

Start with the physical materials. Use the screen to inspect, compare or test a prediction. Keep one worthwhile question going; there is no timer or compulsory sequence.
