Sprouts.

A few dots. One line at a time. Outthink your opponent.

2 PLAYERS · SAME DEVICE
Player 1’s turn
Number = connections remainingGrey = full

Your board

Start with two dots for a short game. No timer, scores or penalties.

Three rules

  1. Join two dots with a line, or loop back to the same dot.
  2. Never cross or retrace a line, or pass through another dot.
  3. Each dot can have at most three connections. A loop uses two.

A new dot appears on every accepted line. Make the last legal move to win.

Try an investigation

Play twice from two dots. Swap who starts. What changed?

Can you make a longer game from the same starting board?

Watch the total connections remaining. Why does it fall by exactly one each turn?

Extension: starting from n dots, explain why a game has at most 3n − 1 moves.
