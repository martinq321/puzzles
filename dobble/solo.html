Maths Wednesdays / Two-player match

Spot the shared symbol.

Both players see the same pair of cards, mixed up. Tap the symbol that appears on both cards in your area. First correct tap: +1 point. Wrong tap: −1 point (minimum zero). Play through the whole deck.
