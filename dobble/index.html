Maths Wednesdays / Fano plane

Make your own matching cards.

Each point is a symbol. Each line makes a card using its three points. Any two lines meet at exactly one point—so any two cards share exactly one symbol.

1. Drag symbols onto vertices

Drag a symbol onto a point, or tap a symbol then a point. Dropping onto an occupied point swaps the symbols. Every linked card updates automatically.

Tap a line or a card to highlight its three points. The circle counts as a line too. Tap a point to find its three cards.
