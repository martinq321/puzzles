This idealised model allows the endpoints to pivot freely on matching rings. It is not a simulation of the forces or joints in a physical scissor mechanism.

What shape is it?

For ring radius R = 1 and twist α, the midpoint radius is cos(α/2). Fixed rods have length L = 3, so ring separation is H = √(9 − 4 sin²(α/2)). With fixed rod length off, H = 2.6 and the rods change length.

For nonzero twist below 180°, the surface is a one-sheet hyperboloid of revolution. At zero twist it is a cylinder. The second family uses the opposite twist. These are two families on the same continuous surface; discrete endpoint sets need not coincide.
