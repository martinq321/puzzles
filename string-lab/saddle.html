Drag to rotate. Add a second family of strings, then take a horizontal slice.

Try it

Find a view where one family looks straight. Does the surface bend up and down at the same time? What shape appears in a slice?

Make it with your hands

Make a four-sided frame with two opposite corners raised and the other two lowered. Mark equally spaced points on opposite edges and connect corresponding points with taut thread. Add the other family in a second colour.

Start by looking along one thread. Then rotate the whole model. This activity can remain entirely observational.

What shape is it?

This is a hyperbolic paraboloid, modelled by z = bxy for −1 ≤ x,y ≤ 1. Fix x and z varies linearly with y; fix y and z varies linearly with x. Both string families are straight.

A horizontal cut z = c gives xy = c/b, a hyperbola when b and c are nonzero. At c = 0 it gives two intersecting straight lines. At b = 0 the surface is flat. Diagonal vertical sections are parabolas.
