Make it with your hands

Arrange 12 counters or square tiles into rectangles: 1 × 12, 2 × 6, 3 × 4, and the turned versions. Plot their top-right corners. Keep the area fixed while changing the shape.

To make tangent strings for a point (x, y), join the axis points (2x, 0) and (0, 2y). Integer factor pairs therefore use integer nail positions. Other slider settings can give noninteger intercepts, shown on screen.

What shape is it?

The corners satisfy xy = K, or y = K/x: one branch of a rectangular hyperbola. The positive branch is shown; a second branch lies where both coordinates are negative.

The string through (2x, 0) and (0, 2y) touches the hyperbola at (x, y). Its intercept product is 4K. Compare this fixed-product rule with the fixed-sum rule on the first page.
