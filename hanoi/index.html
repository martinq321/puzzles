Tower of Hanoi

Move the tower from A to C. Drag the top disc onto another peg.

To play, download this file and open it in Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox. Some file previews display the board but block the game controls.

0 moves · Minimum 7

Drag a disc, or tap a source peg and then a destination.

One disc at a time. A larger disc cannot sit on a smaller disc. You can also use the peg buttons with touch or keyboard. Hint demonstrates one move and returns it. Animate solution starts from A and restores your game afterwards. Turn your device sideways for larger discs.
