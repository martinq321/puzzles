Reading the clues

Black = exact match

Right colour, right hole.

White = colour match

Right colour, different hole.

The little clue pegs are a total. Their order does not tell you which holes they describe.

What about repeated colours?Colours can be used more than once. Each peg can earn only one clue. If the code has one blue and your guess has two blues, at most one of those blues earns a clue.

Hints, controls & offline use

Tap a hole to edit it, then choose a colour. The highlight moves to the next empty hole.

Keyboard: select a hole, then use 1–6 for colours, arrow keys to move and Enter to check.

Hints use all your previous clues. The guarantee depends on the board settings. Read the strategy note below. Your own guesses may take longer.

* The 6-colour, 5-hole optimum uses the later reported exhaustive-search result. For 5 colours / 6 holes the reported optimum is 6, but this app does not certify a six-guess strategy. For 6 colours / 6 holes no exact optimum is asserted.

This file works offline. Keep it with your other Maths Wednesdays activities. A new game starts when you reopen it. Keep Mastermind_Strategy_Tree.html in the same folder to use the tree link.
