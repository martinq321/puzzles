A shared project, at your pace

Introduce the river farm first: “What needs to travel together?” Use physical cards if preferred. The goal is getting everything across safely; the shortest solution is an optional investigation.

Swap roles: one person chooses cargo, the other checks the bank being left behind. A crossing hint shows one legal next move from the current position.

The delivery challenge begins with three destinations. All routes are possible; compare their lengths. Choose more stops when wanted. The exact best target is calculated by checking all possible visiting orders.

Power costs — optional extension

Each leg has a whole-number distance. Compare adding those distances with adding their squares or cubes. These are invented energy costs, not square kilometres or a physical fuel model. The Power costs map is chosen so the shortest route by distance is not the best under squared costs.

Ask: “Would several smaller numbers help?” and “Can you find a route that wins under one rule but loses under another?” The page expands the sum and compares one route under all three rules. Local crossing instructions and sums remain visible.

Completed projects are saved only on this device. There are no accounts, online scores or student records.
