SMALL MOVES / GOOD IDEASMake it yours — swap roles and set a challenge.

One idea is enough.

Begin with the smallest setting. Demonstrate once, then invite a turn. Repeat, change one thing, or finish whenever it feels right.

Cargo

Notice whether quantities are recognised or counted. Try loading together, then swap who chooses the order. Use real blocks alongside the screen.

Routes

Notice how pieces are rotated and checked. A working short road is enough; extra tiles are optional. Copy a route with cards on the table.

Signals

Keep the reference visible first. Hide it only by choice. Notice whether the sequence is copied one light at a time or in groups. Let the learner design your pattern.

Observe: what was independent, what help worked, and what they wanted to repeat. This is exploration, not a formal level test.
