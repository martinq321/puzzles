3D Rigidity Lab

Build bar-and-joint structures in three dimensions. Bars keep the length they had when created; rotate the view to inspect the structure.

No joint selected Move selected joint precisely:

Dragging a joint moves it in the current screen plane. Use ±z or the coordinate boxes for depth. After every move, the constraint solver restores the original bar lengths.

Controls

Move joint: drag a free joint in the screen plane.

Rotate view: drag anywhere to orbit; mouse wheel zooms.

Add joint: click empty space. It is placed in the plane through the origin facing the camera.

+ / − Bar: click two joints. If already connected, the bar is removed.
