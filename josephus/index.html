Which card stays?

Keep one. Remove one. Carry on until only one card remains.

Start counting at position 1: keep position 1, remove position 2. Keep counting through the remaining cards; after the last position, wrap around to the first remaining card. Positions stay fixed when a card leaves. Changing the arrangement does not restart the game.

Gold outline = where counting starts next. Faded cards = removed. Green outline = last card.
